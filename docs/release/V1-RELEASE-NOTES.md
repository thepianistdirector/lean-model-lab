## What the product does

The research workflow now connects a source-backed technique catalog and registered proposal to a frozen model/workload/protocol, actual CPU model execution, independent quality checks, full attempt costs, portable evidence inspection, supported interruption/recovery and verified export. The exact candidate03 source ZIP and archive06 completed that path in a fresh Python 3.12.14 virtual environment and a new same-host worktree. All 19 commands passed, including 32 native comparison responses, an intentional crash, torn-journal repair and eight replacement responses; both pre-crash responses remain. This is a local clean-environment check with reused admitted weights/backend. Its adverse task quality remains visible.

Supported mechanisms include the established prefix-cache control, deterministic context selection and causal controls, cache/slice interactions, a fixed gate and an implemented small empirical acceptance-controller training/deployment path. The controller learns from actual full/sliced development responses and preserves fit/calibration provenance. It does not train or fine-tune the underlying LLM. The final research reports show that its fitted controller selects full context throughout confirmation and produces no learned efficiency advance.

Recipes retain exact model shards, tokenizer/template, backend and implementation identities. Each offered request and unsuccessful attempt remains in quality and cost accounting. Same-input output parity and changed-input exact-answer quality are separate contracts. Ineligible studies retain descriptive measurements while suppressing efficiency claims.

The offline workbench exposes original/transformed inputs, actual token/output evidence, decisions, failures, latency and setup costs. Summary cards show correct responses within both frozen latency targets, and attempt tables show qualified counts and goodput alongside throughput. Distribution now supports a small index, a separate workbench/evidence ZIP per study, and complete investigation ZIPs with source, exact producers and flattened export directories. Portable analysis entrypoints accept explicit evidence paths and fresh outputs.

## Research and limits

The [findings overview](FINDINGS-OVERVIEW.md) tracks the three investigations and their actual confirmation/reproduction states. The retained 0.5B benchmark remains below its frozen 95% quality threshold and fails concurrency-four parity. The retained 14B control passes its own measured comparison; it is a device-specific control with disclosed development history, not a new architecture or universal gain. New prospective results remain separate from those histories.

Synthetic key-copy and record-language studies do not establish general language competence. A valid symbolic dependency slice does not guarantee an unchanged or correct model response. Timing differences with changed outputs and failed quality are not quality-qualified efficiency gains. Energy remains unavailable without a sensor. Agent reproduction is process separation, not external human or institutional validation.

Service and full-attempt timing include coordinator scheduling and resource monitoring. The separate reproduction records larger gaps outside active request intervals and identifies whole-workspace traversal as a plausible operational confound, without establishing causality. Read the [timing guidance](../COMPATIBILITY-AND-TROUBLESHOOTING.md) and the final reproduction report alongside the original ratios.

The [cost-scope guide](COST-ACCOUNTING.md) distinguishes attempt, process, queue and common-allocation time. The external delivery's final accounting record extends the observed preparation cutoff while preserving original producer clocks and charging unattributed intervals.
