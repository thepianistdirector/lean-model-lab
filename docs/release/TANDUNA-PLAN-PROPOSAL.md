# Adopt the bounded v1.0 delivery plan and retain the broader research programme

Prepared proposal for [Lean Model Lab on Tanduna](https://tanduna.com/projects/lean-model-lab). Not submitted, voted on or accepted. The existing initial-roadmap proposal remains a separate historical record.

## Proposed decision

Adopt the current canonical roadmap as the project's planning structure: 249 tasks, 32 waves and 472 dependency edges. Preserve all historical task identities, acceptance requirements and evidence. The bounded v1.0 scope occupies Waves 30 and 31; earlier analytical, training, advanced-kernel and broader research programmes retain their own incomplete requirements.

Wave 30 connects the mechanism catalog, model/workload admission, supported compositions, causal proposal, frozen quality and cost rules, immutable study specification and actual candidate execution to a demonstrated complete workflow. Wave 31 contains the quality benchmark, substantive candidate investigation, systems challenge, separate reproduction, clean-package verification and exact release preparation.

The accompanying [canonical plan](https://github.com/thepianistdirector/lean-model-lab/blob/main/plan/tasks.json), [generated roadmap](https://github.com/thepianistdirector/lean-model-lab/blob/main/ROADMAP.md), [task contracts](https://github.com/thepianistdirector/lean-model-lab/blob/main/TASKS.md) and [Tanduna export](https://github.com/thepianistdirector/lean-model-lab/blob/main/plan/tanduna-export.json) provide the full task definitions and evidence references. They must be attached or linked at the exact source revision approved for publication. Local runtime or automated evidence is not a native platform completion, a passed human review or an accepted public plan.

## Evidence and boundaries

The three original finite investigations are complete and retain their unsuccessful cases. Their [findings overview](https://github.com/thepianistdirector/lean-model-lab/blob/main/docs/release/FINDINGS-OVERVIEW.md), manuscripts, executable specifications and raw evidence explain the measured effects and limits. Separate reproduction and final distribution verification have their own receipts; use their actual final state when submitting this proposal.

The v1 application supports bounded CPU inference studies and a small traced empirical acceptance controller. It does not establish underlying LLM training, a new architecture, general language competence, universal efficiency or measured energy savings. The broader training programme remains separate.

Acceptance of a roadmap does not authorize new spending, experiments, publication, outreach or changes to frozen evidence. Every future study needs authorized resources, a prospectively frozen protocol and appropriate quality/cost evidence. Public release, external first use, qualified methodology review and accessibility observations remain separate decisions and gates.

## Proposed review

Review whether the bounded v1 task chain reflects the delivered product and evidence, whether the three findings are distinct and appropriately scoped, and whether remaining work is visible without falsely completing the broader programme. Record the actual submission identifier, revision, review outcome and decision through Tanduna's supported workflow. Do not manufacture task completion, votes or acceptance from repository statuses.
