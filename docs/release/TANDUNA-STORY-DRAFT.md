Lean Model Lab connects a research proposal to a frozen experiment, real CPU model execution and portable evidence. Researchers can compare registered mechanisms with explicit quality rules, inspect original and transformed inputs, retain unsuccessful attempts, recover supported interruptions and export the full study. A source-backed mechanism catalog helps frame hypotheses and choose comparators; catalog inclusion does not establish local support or novelty.

## Three bounded findings

The quality benchmark tests prefix-cache serving under an unchanged exact-answer and output-parity contract. Its fresh 14B confirmation answers 127 of 128 prompts correctly in each arm and repetition, with matching output tokens. Median service throughput is 4.123 times the baseline across two balanced pairs; full-attempt efficiency is 3.912 times. Neither arm meets the frozen joint interactive-latency target on any response. These are measurements on one shared CPU host, not a general speedup or interactive-service guarantee. The earlier 0.5B quality and parity failure remains in the evidence.

The candidate investigation tests whether symbolic dependency selection and a fitted acceptance controller can preserve useful model answers. On 128 confirmation tables, slicing answers 34 correctly versus 23 for full context, but changes 13 correct answers into errors. Even the oracle restricted to the observed full/slice outputs reaches only 47 correct answers. The actually trained and calibrated controller refuses all omissions and retains the failing full-context baseline. This is a negative result about the tested mechanism and workload; it is neither a new compression architecture nor an LLM training advance.

The systems study challenges omission when a strong baseline already reuses the table prefix. Five cases retain 1,600 responses across two model scales and two context sizes. Slicing processes more new tokens on warm queries for every held-out table. In the held-out 14B comparison, accuracy falls from 73 to 55 correct answers out of 128 while outputs become shorter and wall time falls. Every case fails the unchanged quality gate, so lower descriptive time is not a quality-qualified efficiency win.

## Inspect and reproduce

The prepared distribution contains a portable Python CLI, source, three complete research packages, individual workbenches, raw inventories, exact original producers, analysis scripts, figures and technical manuscripts. A complete ZIP preserves the offline index and its links. Native execution uses the documented Linux x86_64 / Python 3.12.14 CPU lane; pinned model weights and the native backend are obtained separately under their own licenses.

The exact application passed a new same-host worktree and fresh Python environment check, including real inference, export, an intentional crash, journal repair and restart. Automated browser checks cover keyboard task segments, visible focus, native 200% zoom and narrow reflow. A separate agent completed three retained-cell repetitions totaling 1,536 new native observations. Its report identifies exact original/new comparisons, substantial timing variation, all costs and the limits of this same-host process separation. None of these checks is an external person's observation or qualified human review.

Original project source, documentation and synthetic data are AGPL-3.0-only. Third-party material retains its own notices. No model weights or native backend binary are redistributed. Synthetic record and key-copy tasks do not establish general language competence. Energy, thermal and monetary savings are unmeasured.

## Contribute evidence

Useful next contributions include an independently documented first use on supported resources, a methodology review of the three findings, a screen-reader and keyboard review of the actual artifacts, or a prospectively frozen workload or bounded mechanism that challenges a reported limitation. Keep unsuccessful attempts, original quality rules and complete costs. Do not submit private prompts or unlicensed weights.
