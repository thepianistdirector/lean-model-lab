## Researcher first use

One researcher who did not develop the platform should obtain the approved package through its actual public download, verify its manifest, and follow [the first-run guide](../FIRST-RUN-V1.md) on a supported Linux x86_64 / Python 3.12 environment. Record whether this is a separate physical host, a container, a virtual machine or another process on the development host. Only an observed separate host supports that description.

The researcher chooses their own authorized finite CPU, memory, disk and time allocation. The original development allocation grants no additional resources. Use the admitted small-model development lane for first use: obtain and verify the documented model/backend, freeze a proposal and recipe, execute both arms, inspect the raw attempts and quality/cost result, export evidence, and exercise the documented interrupted-attempt recovery. A failing task-quality gate is an acceptable observed result; missing attempts or undocumented developer intervention are usability defects.

Retain exact package/runtime/model hashes, environment, commands, elapsed costs, failures, assistance needed and resulting export. Never send credentials, private prompts or model weights. Consent is required before attributing a review or quoting a person. A single observed first use does not establish general usability or a success rate.

## Methodology review

One reviewer with practical model-serving or experimental ML experience should read the three manuscripts alongside their executable specifications, original producer identities, raw inventories and reanalysis. The same person may perform first use if qualified; record the roles explicitly.

The review should assess whether the findings are distinct and supported; whether frozen quality, parity and latency criteria are interpreted correctly; whether complete costs and unfavorable attempts remain; whether sample/group dependence and development-to-confirmation changes are disclosed; and whether claims exceed the synthetic workloads and single shared host. In particular, distinguish batch throughput from interactive latency, symbolic preservation from model correctness, and a fitted routing refusal from an impossibility result for all learned controllers. Record each substantive objection with an evidence location and the effect on the claim. Agreement is qualified feedback, not peer review or institutional validation.

Assess coordinator and resource-monitor overhead when interpreting service and full-wall ratios. The reproduction reports time outside active request intervals and identifies periodic whole-workspace traversal as a plausible operational confound. Output agreement, measured timing differences and evidence for a causal explanation are separate questions.

## Accessibility review

One reviewer experienced with keyboard and screen-reader use should inspect the exact included workbenches and distribution index. Exercise the skip link, navigation, seven labelled selectors, reset, live counts, request disclosures, failure/cost tables and download links. Check visible focus, sensible traversal, native 200% zoom and narrow reflow; record the browser, assistive technology and actual viewport. Additional 320px/400% reflow, forced-color and no-script checks must be described as observed or untested, never inferred from markup.

Retain concrete task outcomes and reproducible issues. Automated browser evidence and an agent's visual review do not substitute for these observations or establish accessibility certification.

Start from the [unfilled review record](REVIEW-RECORD-TEMPLATE.json) and save a new dated record for each actual review. Blank fields and the template's presence provide no evidence of participation or success. Bind the exact downloaded artifact before recording observations.

Use separate statuses for invited, consented, attempted, completed with assistance, completed without assistance and unable to complete. For each role retain the exact artifact, scope, observations, discrepancies and permitted attribution. Do not manufacture participants, credentials, affiliations, endorsements or authorship.
