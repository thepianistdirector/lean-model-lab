## Immediate control plane and next experimental branch

Wave 30 supports the root-owned `research.py`, `research_catalog.py`, `research_proposals.py` and CLI `research catalog/templates/propose/validate/freeze/verify/compose/compile`. It is inert: no model loading, training runner, arbitrary kernel execution or new backend feature is authorized by a catalog entry, proposal, readiness result or compiler output.

The proposal contract binds closest prior work, overlap/technical delta and unresolved novelty questions; strongest applicable baselines and tested combinations; causal predictions, ablations and adversarial falsifiers; independently versioned quality evaluation; effect/uncertainty decisions; development/confirmation boundaries; full resource/search costs and stopping rules. Training scopes remain distinct from each other and from inference. Exact-key/token equivalence is reserved for the existing cache control, not general capability evaluation.

Freeze includes **the source catalog bytes** with proposal/experiment definitions. Existing frozen meaning cannot silently change with the current registry. Readiness reports missing implementation/admission/evidence separately from valid metadata. Compile accepts only the exact registered prefix-cache infrastructure control and emits an existing v2 recipe preserving its invariants; it does not execute or admit a proposal.

Wave 30's bounded links name broader tasks without replacing them. Wave 31 is the next **later-0.x experimental branch**, with focused prerequisites for a bounded local experiment. The six newly proposed tasks use the following edges; all accepted/source tasks and their original chains remain intact.

Each task explicitly requires its applicable reproduced reference and strong baseline controls, full cost accounting, lawful data, numerical correctness, finite resource limits, frozen independent quality and protected confirmation boundaries. Demonstrated isolation must precede untrusted execution. A device, kernel or distributed extension must meet its applicable admission gates when attempted. Completing unrelated entire catalogs, advanced optimizer/kernel programmes, remote orchestration or publication is not a prerequisite for a bounded local prototype.

Inference and training prototype evidence is independently useful; neither prototype waits for the other. The programme-level frontier task requires both while retaining their separate scope-specific decisions. A cheap failure is a valid research decision; a favorable small result cannot establish unmeasured scaling or independent confirmation. The root's current 0.4 mathematical functional falsifier is narrower than W31-T01 and does not by itself complete that mechanism-specific research acceptance.

This review removes twelve edges only from the newly proposed Wave 31 design (27 proposed edges become 15). It does not delete any of the 442 pre-existing edges, change accepted prerequisites or relax their completion/evidence rules.
